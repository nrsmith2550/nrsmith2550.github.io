Wedding Planner

Your wedding, planned to your date.

Wedding Planner is a Google Sheets template and a companion desktop app for Mac and Windows. Enter your names, wedding date and budget, and it builds a complete plan fitted to the time you have.

What it includes

  • A 72-task checklist with due dates calculated from your wedding date and planning window
  • A budget split across 12 categories, tracking estimates, actual costs, payments and balances
  • A vendor tracker with book-by dates, plus questions to ask and red flags for 13 vendor types
  • A day-of timeline built around your ceremony time, and a month-by-month planning roadmap
  • A guest list for RSVPs, meal choices, tables, gifts and thank-you notes

How the app uses Google

When you click "Connect Google account" in the desktop app, it asks for permission to create and edit files it creates in your Google Drive (the drive.file permission). It uses that permission to create your wedding planner spreadsheet in your Drive and to save the changes you make in the app to that spreadsheet. It cannot see or change any other files in your Drive.

The app runs on your own computer. Your planner data is stored only in your own Google Drive and on your computer. Read the full privacy policy.
