Wrestling Coach Planner · Let's Wrassle Apparel

Run your season from one place.

Wrestling Coach Planner is a Google Sheets template and a companion desktop app for Mac and Windows, sold by Let's Wrassle Apparel. Enter your team and season dates, and it builds your whole season around them.

What it includes

  • A season plan of coaching tasks dated from your first practice, first competition, postseason and state finals
  • A roster with minimum weights, lowest eligible weight class and weekly descent limits based on NFHS rules
  • A weigh-in log and a lineup builder that checks each starter's weight
  • A schedule of duals and tournaments, individual match results and team points
  • A practice calendar with a drill library, plus a team budget and fundraising tracker

How the app uses Google

When you click "Connect Google account" in the desktop app, it asks for permission to create and edit files it creates in your Google Drive (the drive.file permission). It uses that permission to create your season planner spreadsheet in your Drive and to save the changes you make in the app to that spreadsheet. It cannot see or change any other files in your Drive.

The app runs on your own computer. Your team's data is stored only in your own Google Drive and on your computer. Read the full privacy policy.
